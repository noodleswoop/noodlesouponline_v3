## -い adjectives
**い becomes くて**      

ex: sweet and spicy     
甘くて辛い

## -な adjectives
**な becomes で**    

ex: convenient and cheap      
便利で安い

ex: He is a teacher and a student.     
彼は先生で生徒です。
