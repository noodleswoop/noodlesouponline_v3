from "A Dictionary of Basic Japanese Grammar"

1. a particle which lists things exhaustively
	1. "and"
	2. use it when you're listing everything. if you're not listing everything, use [や](/japanese/や)
2. a particle marking the noun phrase which maintains a reciprocal relationship with the subject of a clause
	1. with; as; from
3. a particle which marks a quotation, sound, or the manner in which someone/something does something
	1. that; with the sound of; in the manner of

it can also be used as a conjunction:
4. a subordinate conjunction which marks a condition that brings about an uncontrollable event or state
	1. if; when
but we're focusing on the particle version right now.

for other connective particles, see [し](/japanese/し), [て](/japanese/て), and [や](/japanese/や)
