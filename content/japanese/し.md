(noun/adjective/adverb) し (noun/adjective/adverb): し functions as a connector particle. has a bit of a silly connotation, like "like" in english. can connect two phrases/sentences.

for other connective particles, see [と](/japanese/と), [て](/japanese/て), and [や](/japanese/や)
